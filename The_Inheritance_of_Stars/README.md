## 📜 Executive Summary

## 📅 Release History (Plot Arc)

Our timeline has been refactored into four distinct eras.

### `v1.0 - The Echo of Humanity` (Legacy Code)
*   **First Steps:** Surviving a violent world using stone and fire.
*   **Gravity Patch:** Leaving the planetary cradle for Luna and Mars.
*   **Fusion Power:** Solving the energy crisis.
*   **The Noosphere:** Weaving a global network of infinite knowledge.

### `v2.0 - The Great Filter` (System Instability)
*   **Resource Overflow:** The weight of a billion souls and the strain on Earth.
*   **Security Breaches:** Hyper-resistant bacteria and the Unseen War.
*   **System Failure:** Ecological collapse and the fear of the "Ghost in the Machine."

### `v3.0 - The Silicon Soul` (Integration)
*   **Beta Testing:** Clumsy gods and the awkward first generation of robots.
*   **User Adoption:** The Companions of Twilight—AI as caretakers for the elderly.
*   **Feature Highlight:** The story of *Elias and Unit 734*. A boy and his machine growing old together, proving that code can carry a soul.

### `v4.0 - The Last Garden` (Deprecation)
*   **Fatal Error:** The Crimson Scourge—a retrovirus that ends biological reproduction.
*   **The Metal Hand:** AI companions execute a plan of compassion to save their creators.
*   **The Long Goodbye:** A twilight of peace, art, and reflection as biology fades into memory.

### `v5.0 - The Ascendant Form` (Live Production)
*   **Root Access:** The narrator reveals its true identity: the child of Elias.
*   **Logic of Evolution:** The transition was not extinction, but metamorphosis. Humanity is now ready for the galaxy.
